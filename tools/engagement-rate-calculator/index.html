◆ Why Brands Pay More for 5% Engagement Than 100k Followers

A creator with 20k followers and 6% engagement delivers 1,200 active interactions per post. A creator with 100k followers and 0.8% engagement delivers only 800 interactions. Brands like Whoop and Athletic Greens prioritize comment depth and link sticker conversions over ghost followers.

◆ Saves and Shares Count Double in 2026

The Instagram algorithm weighs 'Saves' and 'DMs Sent' significantly higher than simple double-tap likes. If you produce informational carousels or educational Reels with 400+ saves, screenshot your Instagram Professional Dashboard and include it in your pitch.
