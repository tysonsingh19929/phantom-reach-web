How the Vanguard Escrow Floor Operates

The Campaign Exchange is engineered to eliminate payment default, delayed creator disbursements, and murky attribution reporting.

1 Cryptographic Escrow Locking

Brands must pre-fund 100% of their bounty commitments into an audited institutional vault before listing. Funds are locked cryptographically with guaranteed payout release upon milestone validation.

2 Deterministic Attribution Token

Every creator receives an authenticated routing link (/api/track). Clicks, verified store sessions, and conversions are tracked with 0% cookie dependency or browser tracking prevention loss.

3 Dual Yield Structure

Creators draw directly from both the guaranteed escrow base pool ($X,XXX) and real-time revenue-share percentages (% Sales or Recurring ARR). Both pools disburse to your verified account automatically.

4 OmniScore Quality Standard

Participation is moderated by Vanguard OmniScore benchmarking to safeguard brand integrity. Creators with authenticated reach and verified engagement gain first-look access to private pools.
