Help & Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers about how sponsorship rates work, how brands pay you upfront, how to pitch local businesses, and how tracking links work.

How does the Vanguard Creator Rate Card & Valuation Engine calculate sponsorship pricing?

Our calculator analyzes your real follower count, engagement rate, recent video views, and audience location. It calculates recommended pricing for dedicated Reels, Stories, and content packages in both US Dollars ($) and Indian Rupees (₹) so you know exactly what to charge brands.

What is the Vanguard Campaign Exchange and how does the Verified Escrow Pool work?

The Campaign Exchange is a place where brands post paid sponsorship opportunities. To protect creators, brands deposit the full campaign budget into a secure escrow account before you make any content. Once you post your content as agreed, your payment is released to you immediately. You never have to worry about unpaid invoices or chasing brands for payment.

How can creators use Google Leads Tools to pitch local businesses?

Creators use Vanguard Google Leads Tools to find local businesses in their city (like cafes, gyms, clinics, and shops). The tool extracts verified owner emails, phone numbers, and websites so creators can reach out directly with their media kit.

How does Instagram Leads discovery help creators find brand collaborations?

The Instagram Leads tool searches by location, hashtag, or category to find active businesses and brands on Instagram. It gathers contact emails, phone numbers, and profile engagement so you can easily spot businesses looking for creator partnerships.

How does YouTube channel performance scoring differ from basic view counts?

Instead of just looking at total subscriber counts, Vanguard YouTube analytics evaluates recent view consistency, upload frequency, and audience engagement. This gives sponsors and creators a clear picture of real reach when negotiating video sponsorships.

Can creators on TikTok and YouTube participate in the Vanguard Campaign Exchange?

Yes. You can share your personal campaign tracking link on any platform including TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn, or newsletters. When viewers click your link or buy, you are credited automatically.

How do Creator Collabs and Syndicate Co-Pitches increase brand deal win rates?

When 2 or 3 creators in the same city or niche pitch a brand together, they offer a combined audience that appeals to larger brand budgets. Our collaboration hub provides ready-to-send pitch templates so creators can team up and land bigger deals.

How does LinkedIn B2B lead generation work for agency owners and creator consultants?

Vanguard LinkedIn search helps identify marketing managers, brand partnership heads, and agency directors at companies. It extracts company details and contact coordinates so you can pitch corporate sponsorships and consulting services.

Is there any sign-up fee or platform commission for creators joining escrow campaigns?

Vanguard charges 0% platform commission on creator base earnings from verified escrow pools. Brands fund the escrow budget upfront, and 100% of the agreed deliverables fee and tracked sales commissions are paid directly to you without agency middleman cuts.

How does Vanguard AI search optimization benefit creators and brands?

All creator rate benchmarks, public media kits, and brand campaigns are formatted with clean structured data. This helps your profile and listings get discovered accurately by AI search engines including Google, Perplexity, and ChatGPT.
